Chapter 11: Difference-in-Differences and Event Studies

1 Runtime note

This chapter pins WebR 0.5.9 (R 4.5). That runtime has a mutually compatible WebAssembly package chain for did, its fastglm dependency, and fixest. R runs inside the browser; no local installation or Binder session is needed.

The live settings are intentionally small: 400 units and 99 multiplier- bootstrap repetitions. They are appropriate for a quick classroom demonstration, not final empirical inference. For an actual project, use a native R environment, restore the full sample, and use at least 999 bootstrap repetitions (often more after checking simulation or Monte Carlo error).

2 Generate staggered-adoption panel data

Some units first adopt in periods 4, 6, or 8; others never adopt (G = 0). Effects grow with exposure time and differ by cohort.

Untreated outcomes obey parallel trends by construction. Every treatment effect is positive, but effects are heterogeneous across cohorts and exposure lengths.
